“Can matter explain the witness who knows matter?”

The inquiry

From the first elements to the final surrender

The Supreme Person moves from stellar matter and the chemistry of life into cells, bodies, minds, societies and spiritual consciousness. It places modern scientific questions beside Sāṃkhya, yoga and bhakti without asking either language to pretend it is the other.

The central distinction is simple: nature is what changes; consciousness is what knows change. The book follows that distinction toward a personal understanding of the Divine.

## Longer post

Prakriti and Purusha begin with a distinction that reaches into everyday life.

Prakriti is the changing field: body, sensation, emotion, memory, personality and the forces of nature. Purusha is the conscious witness by which those changes are known.

This does not ask us to deny the body or avoid responsibility. It asks whether every passing state deserves to define the whole person. Anger can be known before it becomes speech. Fear can be felt without becoming identity. Action can continue without total bondage to outcome.

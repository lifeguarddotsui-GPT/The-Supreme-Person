## Longer post

Many spiritual books begin by asking the reader to accept an answer. The Supreme Person begins with questions: How does life arise? Why is there consciousness? What makes someone a person? Can action, knowledge and devotion lead beyond ego?

Its 36 chapters move through a three-part structure: creation, preservation and transformation. Science helps describe matter, evolution and living systems. Indian philosophy supplies a vocabulary for nature, the witnessing self, disciplined action, knowledge and devotion. The goal is not to force science and spirituality into artificial agreement, but to let each sharpen the questions asked of the other.
